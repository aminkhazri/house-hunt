        How we picked: we only included homes that were still for sale on Redfin on November 14. Then we ranked them by neighborhood safety grade first, then by value (how much house you get for the price), the price itself, school ratings, size and age, and our own notes from looking at each listing.

        Safety grades come from CrimeGrade.org for each home's zip code (A+ is safest; the safety score runs from 0 to 1000) and are checked again every week. Photos and prices come from Redfin, and prices are checked again every morning. Our list is kept in a Google Sheet, so a home removed here comes off the Sheet too.
